About Birsa Institute of Technology, Sindri

Established in 1949 by the Government of Bihar (now Jharkhand) · Accredited by AICTE and affiliated to Jharkhand University of Technology (JUT), Ranchi

1949 Established
450 Acres Campus Area
35,000+ Alumni Base

Chronicle of Excellence (1949 – Present)

75+ Years Legacy
1949

Foundation & Inception

The institute commenced operations at Patna as the 'College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering' under the Department of Applied Science, Govt. of Bihar.

1950

Relocation to Industrial Heartland Sindri

Shifted to its present sprawling 450-acre lush green campus in Sindri, Dhanbad, situated amidst India's coal, steel, and heavy fertilizer industrial corridor.

1953 - 1960

Expansion into Core Engineering

Expanded to introduce Chemical, Metallurgical, Civil, Mining, and Production Engineering, emerging as eastern India's pioneer technical powerhouse.

2000

Establishment under Government of Jharkhand

Following the reorganization of Bihar, the premier institute became the flagship engineering institution of the newly formed state of Jharkhand.

2017

Siemens Center of Excellence (CoE)

Established the ₹50+ Crore state-of-the-art Siemens Center of Excellence equipped with 14 industrial research labs for Industry 4.0 automation.

2024 - 2026

Platinum Jubilee & Next-Gen Modernization

Celebrated 75 glorious years of engineering triumph; expanded state-of-the-art AICTE Idea Labs, smart lecture complexes, and cutting-edge computing infrastructure.

Institutional Vision

To provide valuable resources for industry and society through excellence in technical education and research, fostering ethically sound engineers and technocrats who drive sustainable socio-economic development.

Institutional Mission

  • To offer world-class undergraduate and postgraduate programs in diverse engineering and scientific domains.
  • To establish state-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities in partnership with global industry leaders.
  • To nurture ethical leadership, entrepreneurship, and an interdisciplinary problem-solving spirit among students.
  • To forge strong collaborations with premier research bodies, industries, and alumni worldwide.

Location & How to Reach

Address: BIT Sindri, P.O. Sindri Institute, Dhanbad, Jharkhand - 828123

By Train: Nearest major railway junction is Dhanbad Junction (DHN), located 28 km away with 24x7 bus and taxi availability. Sindri Town railway station is located 2 km from campus.

By Air: Nearest operational airports are Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport, Durgapur (85 km) and Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi (155 km).

By Road: Well connected via NH-19 (Grand Trunk Road) and state highways from Dhanbad, Bokaro Steel City (40 km), and Asansol (60 km).

B.Tech Admissions

Admissions into 10 engineering disciplines are conducted strictly via JCECEB state counseling based on JEE (Main) Common Rank List (CRL).

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics as compulsory subjects, minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved categories).

Lateral Entry: Diploma engineers admitted directly into 3rd Semester via JCECEB Lateral Entry exam.

M.Tech Admissions

Offered in Machine Design, Heat Power, Power Systems, Process Metallurgy, and Plant Design.

Eligibility: Valid GATE score in respective discipline. Non-GATE candidates considered against vacant seats through JUT entrance test.

Scholarship: AICTE PG Scholarship (₹12,400/month) for GATE-qualified candidates.

Ph.D Doctoral Studies

Full-time and part-time research programs under Jharkhand University of Technology (JUT) in all engineering and basic science branches.

Eligibility: Master’s degree with minimum 60% marks and qualification in JUT Entrance / UGC-NET / GATE.
